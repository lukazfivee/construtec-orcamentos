Style/medium: high-end product UI design, precise engineering editorial system inspired by commissioning dossiers, technical memoranda, approval sheets and calibrated instruments. Constraints: exact Portuguese labels where legible; professional internal application; realistic synthetic demo data only; no external website; no AI features; no cloud dependency messaging beyond offline/local state; no invented logo; no construction-worker photography; no gradients; no glassmorphism; no floating bento cards; no generic blue SaaS dashboard; no watermark.
