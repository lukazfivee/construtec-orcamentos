Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity north-star desktop application comp, landscape 16:10, for an Electron app
Primary request: Design the first operational dashboard/editor screen for “Construtec Orçamentos” in Brazilian Portuguese, using the visual world “Arquivo de Caixas”. This is professional desktop estimation software, not a warehouse illustration or poster.
Structure: full desktop window. At far left, navigation behaves like a vertical archive wall: slim labeled box-end tabs for “Início”, “Propostas”, “Catálogo”, “Clientes”, “Kits”. Across the upper area, recent/open proposals appear as horizontal archive labels: “PA-1054 • REV.00”, “PA-1052 • REV.01”, “PA-1048 • REV.00”. The selected proposal visually slides from its archive into a broad central work surface. Central content shows “Edifício Horizonte”, “Controle de acesso” and a precise editable table of products/services with codes, descriptions, quantities, costs and sale values. A right-side open-folder layer displays “Resumo comercial”, “Custo R$ 18.430,00”, “Venda R$ 26.723,50”, “Resultado bruto R$ 8.293,50”, internal “BDI 1,45”, badge “Preço congelado nesta revisão”, and a decisive “Gerar proposta” action. Include a compact, clearly searchable product drawer with labels for categories such as CFTV, Controle de acesso, Redes.
Style/medium: excellent mature product UI derived from indexed archive boxes, end labels, pressed cardboard, tissue separators and warehouse classification systems. Translate the physical grammar into efficient digital navigation, not decorative 3D scenery. Labels are the core component system; opened layers reveal detail without hiding context.
Palette: burnt orange board #C85F32, warm label paper #F4E9D5, near-black ink #191A18, muted green #55736A for verified states. Use material subtly, maintain high legibility.
Typography: condensed uppercase label headers, modern sans body, monospaced SKU codes and tabular values.
Composition: the editable proposal stays the dominant 60% workspace; archive navigation 16%; summary 24%. One focal endpoint: sale total and “Gerar proposta”.
Constraints: exact Portuguese labels where legible; synthetic demo data only; no logos; no product photos; no literal shoe imagery; no cartoon warehouse; no excessive perspective; no gradients; no glassmorphism; no bento cards; no generic blue SaaS dashboard; no watermark.
