Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity north-star desktop application comp, landscape 16:10, for an Electron app
Primary request: Design the first operational dashboard/editor screen for “Construtec Orçamentos” in Brazilian Portuguese, using the visual world “Caderno de Obra”. This must unmistakably be a polished professional desktop software interface, not a notebook photograph, not a poster.
Structure: full desktop window. A compact dark green top bar with app name, offline/local indicator, global search and user. Along the left, slim physical-style colored index tabs for “Início”, “Propostas”, “Catálogo”, “Clientes”, “Kits”. A large central proposal composition sheet dominates the canvas, headed “PA-1054 • REV.00”, “Edifício Horizonte”, “Controle de acesso”. Show a precise line-item table with codes, descriptions, quantities, unit, cost and sale values. A persistent right margin contains a live pricing calculation column with “Custo R$ 18.430,00”, “Venda R$ 26.723,50”, “Resultado bruto R$ 8.293,50”, internal “BDI 1,45”, and the primary action “Gerar proposta”. Add a visible tag “Preço congelado nesta revisão”. Below, a compact strip of recent proposals.
Style/medium: exceptional product UI design derived from engineering field notebooks, graph paper, technical ink, measured crop marks, sticky index tabs and review annotations. The graph grid is functional and restrained, aligning columns and controls; it must not be a decorative background. Digital precision with tactile field-document character.
Palette: deep field green #20342E, warm off-white paper #F7F1E3, burnt orange #D65F35 for decisive action, blueprint blue #2F638F for links and technical references, charcoal ink.
Typography: readable contemporary sans with a disciplined technical mono for codes and tabular numerals. Clear hierarchy and realistic desktop density.
Composition: central sheet about 65% width, right calculation margin about 22%, navigation tabs very slim. The total and approval action form the clear focal endpoint.
Constraints: exact Portuguese labels where legible; realistic synthetic example data; no invented logo; no photographs; no spiral binding; no fake handwriting as primary text; no gradients; no glassmorphism; no bento-card dashboard; no generic blue SaaS look; no AI features; no watermark.
